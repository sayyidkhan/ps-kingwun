# King Wun: photographic website direction

## Reference and adaptation

The September 2026 review of [SERHANT.](https://serhant.com/) and
[Ryan Serhant's personal site](https://ryanserhant.com/) informed the content
sequence: introduce the person, show a property perspective, explain the
business process, share useful thinking, then provide a next action.
[The Modern House](https://themodernhouse.com/) informed the architectural
photography and restrained editorial presentation.

This version does not claim a brokerage, technology product, venture,
transaction record, client endorsement, or property listing that has not been
supplied and verified. Decision notes are general educational copy for review,
not claimed personal transaction stories. The brief is local and copies text;
it does not send an enquiry.

### Architecture prompt

Create a photographic editorial architecture image for a sophisticated
real-estate personal website. Wide landscape 3:2. A fictional contemporary
tropical courtyard residence: long low travertine pavilion, rich walnut
screens, deep shaded overhangs, floor-to-ceiling glass with a warm interior,
a reflecting pool with soft ripples in foreground, one elegant frangipani
tree and lush restrained tropical planting. Architectural magazine
photography, late afternoon light with sculptural shadows, warm limestone,
bronze, deep natural green and charcoal palette. Carefully framed straight
verticals, incredible believable material textures, physically plausible
detailing. Quiet understated luxury, human scale, no enormous palace.
Composition features a strong diagonal from near stone terrace toward
glowing glazing. No people, signage, text, watermarks, logos, real buildings,
or listing branding. This is illustrative architectural concept photography,
not a property advertised for sale. Output one image only.

### Working portrait prompt

Use case identity-preserve. Generate ONE companion environmental portrait,
landscape 3:2, for King Wun's real estate personal brand. Image 1 is
authoritative actual facial identity, Image 2 establishes wardrobe,
photographic atmosphere and architecture. Keep his real face, hair, clear
round glasses, age, warm skin, full cheeks, natural anatomy; never genericize
or beautify. Scene: candid editorial photograph of him seated at a walnut
work table in a refined tropical architectural studio, reviewing a paper
floor plan with a pencil, a slim open laptop off to one side (screen turned
away). Three-quarter camera view, subject in center-right, looks up warmly
toward photographer with the same recognizable smile as original. Charcoal
unstructured blazer with white open-collar shirt. Architectural light softly
rakes the limestone wall behind him. Green foliage through tall window on
left, restrained bronze details, realistic lived-in work setting with only
a few paper plans and a material sample. Frame waist-up and show both
naturally posed hands and tabletop. Editorial magazine photography with
honest texture, natural facial detail, realistic lighting, coherent
architectural perspective. More candid and intimate than the standing hero,
conveys prepared personable professional. No other people, text, branding,
fake screens, dashboards, watermarks. Fictional illustrative setting, not
documentary evidence of a real transaction. Do not change identity.

## Motion

- Hero image: one gentle 5-second settling zoom; no continuous looping.
- Hero copy: staggered entrances, ending within 1.4 seconds.
- Section elements: 700 ms reveals on intersection; content remains visible
  without JavaScript.
- Photo hover: small scale change, only for hover-capable devices.
- Native expandable notes support keyboard interaction.
- The motion control stops scripted reveals and hero motion.
- Device reduced-motion preferences disable animation and smooth scrolling.
